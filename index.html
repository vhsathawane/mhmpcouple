About us

Hey there! 👋 Welcome to MHMP Couple, a little corner of the internet where we share our love for travel, road trips, delicious food, and unforgettable experiences!

We are Vishal and Shweta Sathawane, a fun-loving couple based in Nagpur, Maharashtra. We tied the knot in 2021 and have been creating beautiful memories together ever since. Vishal is from Nagpur, Maharashtra, while Shweta comes from Chhindwara, Madhya Pradesh. ❤️

🌍 Our Love for Travel

For us, travelling is more than just visiting new places. It's about exploring hidden gems, enjoying scenic road trips, discovering local food, experiencing different cultures, and collecting stories that stay with us forever.

From exploring beautiful destinations to finding cosy cafes and trying mouth-watering local dishes, we love making every journey special. Whether it's a quick weekend getaway or a long road trip, we believe that the best memories are made when we explore the world together.

📸 Why We Started This Blog

We started this blog to share our real travel experiences, favourite destinations, road trip stories, cafe discoveries, food recommendations, and useful travel tips with fellow explorers.

Our goal is to help you discover new places, plan your next adventure, and find inspiration for your own journeys. We want to share not just where we went, but also what we loved, what we discovered, and the little moments that made each trip memorable.

💑 Connecting with Like-Minded Couples

We believe travelling becomes even more special when we connect with people who share the same passion. Through MHMP Couple, we hope to meet like-minded couples who enjoy travelling, exploring new places, going on road trips, and discovering amazing food.

If you are a couple who loves adventures, meaningful connections, and making memories together, you are in the right place! Let's explore, connect, and create beautiful memories together. 👫🌏

❤️ Let's Explore Together!

Follow our journey, get inspired for your next adventure, and be a part of our growing travel-loving community. Don't forget to follow us on Instagram at @mhmp_couple .

Travel together. Explore together. Make memories forever!
